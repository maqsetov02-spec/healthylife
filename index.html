Recommended Warm-Up (5–10 Minutes Before Every Session)

Neck/shoulder rolls, arm circles, hip rotations, dynamic leg swings, light jogging in place, and ankle mobility to raise body temperature and prevent injury.

Recommended Cool-Down (5–10 Minutes After Every Session)

Slow walking to lower heart rate, static hamstring/quad/calf stretches (hold 20s each), child's pose, and deep nasal breathing for muscle recovery.
